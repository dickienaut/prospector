## What changed in the reasoning since yesterday

Yesterday's note recommended **holding** the deploy until the multi-fund question was settled. That
recommendation was **wrong, and the corpus harness is what corrected it**: production at v1.0.4 was
*already* emitting unattributable corroborations. On 2026-07-19 GCPB (`S000106340`) and GINC
(`S000106341`) emitted **byte-identical** `mandate.changed` events from one Goldman Sachs ETF Trust
485APOS — a **two-fund filing with two strategy sections**, both series having received the first.

So holding was not the safe option it appeared to be: it would have kept shipping the same false
records, minus the extraction fix. #118 does not open that hole — it **widens** it (3 suspect firings
→ 8), which is why #122 ships in the same release.
