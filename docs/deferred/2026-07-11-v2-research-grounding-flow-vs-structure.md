## 1. The literature landscape — where the idea sits

- **The methods Prospector uses are mature and validated** (2+ decades). Correlation networks on equities
  (Mantegna minimum spanning trees), community detection for correlation matrices (MacMahon–Garlaschelli, Phys
  Rev X 2015), partial-correlation to strip the market mode (Kenett et al.), RMT / Marchenko-Pastur matrix
  cleaning, PMFG, Infomap/Louvain, and recent dynamic/temporal community detection (modularity-spectral,
  graph-neural). **Implication:** dispersion (spectral bipartition of a cleaned constituent-correlation matrix)
  and xcorr (residual correlation after stripping market beta) are *standard, defensible methodology* — good
  for credibility (reviewers won't question the core method), but **not a novel contribution in themselves.**
- **The *concept* — detect emerging themes ahead of packaging — is the active frontier, academically AND
  commercially.**
  - *Academic (2024–2025):* "concept stocks" clustered by association with emerging disruptive trends, with
    concepts maturing into industries (the Nvidia-enables-self-driving-cars-before-it's-a-sector framing =
    proto-clusters); systems built explicitly because current thematic methods rely on *static ETF compositions
    or expert lists* that are slow to reflect emerging companies.
  - *Commercial:* the largest asset manager is already here — BlackRock states that capturing thematic alpha
    requires **"anticipating new linkages across stocks,"** and runs a live AI-driven ETF (THRO) whose managers
    seek to identify emerging themes and rotate in real time.
  - **Implication:** the core idea is *not novel* — it's a crowded, well-funded frontier. Being there is
    validation that others believe signal exists; it is not a first-mover claim.

## 2. The central open question — flow vs structure (the null hypothesis to beat)

The enemy is **not random noise hiding a signal — it is reflexive *flow* mimicking structure.**
- *"Ponzi Funds"* (arXiv 2405, 2024): a thematic ETF's own **flow-induced price impact** tracked its return
  >40% correlated; the fund's positions were **20× the daily volume** of the underlying stocks, so a 1% inflow
  meant buying ~20% of daily volume — the theme's co-movement was substantially **self-generated by the fund's
  buying**, not underlying reality, and it collapsed after.
- ETF/fund flows as **"dumb money"** (Frazzini–Lamont lineage): initial price pressure then **mean-reversion**;
  flow-driven outperformance is fleeting (months), explained by flow-buying pushing prices beyond fundamentals.
- The field's framing: thematic ETF performance is strongly correlated with investor flows, leaving open
  whether returns reflect **structural growth or transient demand.**

**So the danger is subtler than noise:** an artifact (flow) that *looks like* structure and *reverts*. Any v2
claim of "we detect real emerging structure" must defeat this null, not just beat random noise.

## 3. Why Prospector is unusually well-aimed at beating that null

Three design properties, already in the architecture, are the discriminators the skeptical literature lacks:
1. **Detection among the most liquid large-caps** (S&P sector-SPDR constituents). The Ponzi effect lives in
   *thin* underlyings where a fund's flow is 20× volume; no thematic ETF moves the correlation structure of
   NVDA/AAPL by buying. **Structure is far more separable from flow up there** — the detection universe is
   well-chosen for exactly this reason.
2. **Lead-time *before the vehicle exists* is the discriminator itself.** If the forming cluster is detected
   *before the ETF launches* — before the flow vehicle exists — it *cannot* be that vehicle's flow creating it.
   The recall result (a +593-day lead: structure preceding packaging by ~19 months) is the cleanest possible
   evidence of **structure-ahead-of-flow.**
3. **The pre-registered-falsifier / null-model / FDR discipline is precisely what the debate lacks.** Flow-vs-
   structure is *unresolved* because most approaches can't cleanly separate the two; separating them is what
   this project is built to do.

## 4. The distinctive angle — the possibly-novel operationalization

Most work uses thematic ETFs as *tracking targets* (find stocks similar to an ETF) or as motivation. The
framing that appears distinctive — **not seen in the scan as standard** — is using **ETF/index launches as a
dated "ground-truth timestamp of consensus,"** against which to measure detection **lead time**: *"we detected
the forming cluster N days before the crowd packaged it."* That turns a fuzzy "we detect emerging themes" claim
into a **measurable lead over a dated event**, and it doubles as the flow-vs-structure discriminator (§3.2).
**This operationalization — the launch as the clock — may be the actual contribution, more than the detection.**
(Verify against a proper lit review before claiming novelty.)

## 5. The testable v2 experiment — the historical-launch trajectory backtest

The empirical core of the whole question, tractable with data on hand (Tiingo prices + the launch stream +
holdings). For each historical thematic-ETF launch:
- Reconstruct the basket **as of launch (point-in-time, not revised).**
- Measure, in windows **before and after** launch: (a) the **intra-basket correlation structure** (was the
  cluster forming *pre*-launch?), and (b) the basket's **excess return vs the market** (never raw).
- **Confirming pattern:** structure forming pre-launch + excess return that **persists** post-launch (caught
  real structure early, and it paid).
- **Refuting pattern:** structure and return cohering only *around* launch and **reverting** after (packaging =
  the top; the dumb-money / reflexive-flow signature).

**Guards (bake in, or the result is worthless):** include ETFs that **launched and died** (survivorship — the
packaged-and-failed themes are as informative as the winners); **PIT data** (no look-ahead); **excess-vs-
benchmark always** (a basket that rose with a rising market proves nothing). This is the generalization of the
deferred milestone-3b calibration, pointed at the v2 question.

## 6. Publishability

Not "we can detect emerging themes" (done, commercialized). The contribution would be **a falsifiable test of
whether early structural detection catches genuine structure-ahead-of-flow versus reflexive flow — using
detect-before-the-ETF-exists as the discriminator, with a null model and a measured lead-time / false-discovery
rate.** Publishable **either way:** a positive result is a real finding; a *negative* result ("the early signal
is mostly reflexive flow that reverts") is a contribution to an actively-argued, unresolved debate. The
project's rigor (pre-registered falsifiers, null model, PIT) is exactly what would make either result credible
where most such claims die on overfitting/p-hacking critique. **Bar = a measured, out-of-sample, null-tested
FDR over enough events — which is the same thing as the multi-year long run.** "Publishable" and "the long run
resolves" are one milestone.

## 7. Honest caveat on intuition

The operator's intuition — "there must be signal or they couldn't find things to package" — proves a *theme*
exists (a cluster forms), **not** that it's *tradeable ahead of time*. Structure-exists (yes) and
exploitable-early-signal (open) are different claims. The strongest recent evidence (§2) says much of what
looks like emerging-theme structure is self-inflating flow that mean-reverts. So **"engineered to be able to
lose" is warranted here, not just a posture:** the null (it's all flow) is a serious, well-evidenced contender.
Build the discriminator; let the record answer.
