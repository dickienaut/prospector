# Idea note — the tunable pattern-finder (a what-if, captured for later)

*2026-07-03. Not a plan; a dream worth not losing. Prospector-as-a-product, the reframe underneath it, and the one thing that would make it real rather than dangerous.*

**What it actually is.** The realization: Prospector is a pattern-*finding* machine, not a pattern-*matching* one. A matcher only finds shapes you specified in advance — which is the consensus trap by construction, since you can only match the already-named. A finder detects structure nobody templated yet (Tech×Utilities cohering, Financials decoupling in 2020, a proto-cluster forming) via unsupervised departure-from-baseline. The consequence that opens the product door: the patterns it finds are *objective-neutral*. The 2020 Financials decoupling was real structure; it was "off-thesis" only relative to *my* emerging-tech objective — to a rates or macro trader it's the signal of the year. So the finder is separable from the objective, which means the objective is a *tunable layer* on top of an objective-neutral engine. My Prospector is that engine tuned to one brain (17+ year horizon, emerging tech, over-trading as the enemy). The product would be the same engine, tuned by each user to their own objective.

**Why the niche is empty — corrected.** I'd claimed "no institution shares the objective, no vendor sells to it," and half of that was wrong. Incumbents genuinely can't build it (an institution needs pre-consensus alpha on 1–3 year horizons, so "consensus-but-true is fine" is worthless to them; an engagement-monetizing vendor needs the idea-firehose to *be* the product, so "made you act less" is unsellable). But "incumbents won't build it" ≠ "no one would buy it" — that combination is exactly what an *underserved* market looks like. Offense also sells better than defense: nobody dreams of a smoke detector, everybody wants to find the thing early. And the barrier to publishing has dropped — a static site, trivial hosting, social marketing, a few paying users to cover costs is within reach.

**Why productizing is dangerous — the core tension.** The thing that makes Prospector *good* is the thing that makes it hard to sell: its value is the *restraint* — the oracle ceiling, the reading-list-not-order wall, the anti-engagement cadence, the novelty≠interest refusal to show you what already excites you. It works because it makes you trade less and look harder. A paid product's incentives run the other way — retention wants engagement, engagement wants more alerts, more alerts wants a lower ceiling, a lower ceiling wants the firehose — and the moment you optimize for what makes people renew, you've rebuilt the consensus-chasing hype engine the tool exists to refuse. Sell restraint to someone who wants action and either they churn ("boring") or you give them the firehose and it stops being Prospector. The value evaporates the instant you optimize it for sale.

**The guardrail — the analog-radio principle.** The resolution is tunability like an *old analog radio*, not a digital settings page. A digital tuner offers "disable safety limits: y/n" and someone always clicks yes; an analog dial has hard stops *milled into the housing* — the knob turns freely across the legitimate band and physically cannot go past the ends, and there's no menu to remove the stops because they're the shape of the thing, not software. The user tunes the *free band* (which baskets, which corroboration keywords, what counts as on- vs off-thesis, where thresholds sit within reason). The user cannot reach the *stops* — the anti-spurious discipline: placebo-before-calibration, the recall-independence check, corroboration-is-a-witness-not-a-judge, multiple-comparisons honesty, the structural inertness of off-thesis dispositions. Those must be milled into the housing (structural, the way `off-thesis-real`'s inertness is milled into the fold), never a setting a user can tune away. **Acceptance test for whether a shippable version is still the real thing: can the user reach the stops? If yes, don't ship.** This matters *more* for a product than for my own copy, not less — my version is safe because I hold the anti-fitting discipline in my own head; a stranger doesn't, so for them it has to live *entirely* in the housing.

**The gate — a measured false-discovery rate, not an anecdote.** A fully-tunable pattern-finder is, viewed adversarially, a machine for manufacturing whatever pattern you unconsciously want — turn the thresholds down, widen the baskets, and it will always find *something*, and a human will always tell a story about why the spurious thing is real. So the entire safety question is the *false-discovery rate*, and "it found the Financials-2020 decoupling" is exactly one true positive — it says nothing about how often it surfaces noise. Proving the FDR is low enough is therefore not a phase *after* "prove it works" — it *is* the precondition for the business existing, because a pattern-finder whose spurious rate you can't characterize is precisely the thing that must not be sold to strangers with money. The honest sequence is the real gate: finish the corroboration-hook capstone (first live lag) → calibrate the dispersion sensor (the deferred §3b) → run it live-forward long enough to actually *measure* the noise rate.

**Status.** A what-if, and a genuinely *different* project from the personal tool — captured here so it's buildable later, not started now. Prove the engine finds real patterns (and learn how often it finds fake ones) for *one* objective before building the machine that finds them for anyone's. The next move hasn't changed: the corroboration hook, the capstone that turns the thesis into a measurement. Everything above is downstream of a number the machine hasn't produced yet.
