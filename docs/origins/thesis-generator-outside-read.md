# Thesis Generator — Outside Read & Recommendations

*Companion input to the Decisions Review (dated 2026-06-29). One reader's assessment, written to be dropped into the build thread: what to protect, the one decision worth making now, one structural addition, and the permanent limit to keep named. Stress-test framing — the praise is specific and load-bearing, not decorative. Section references (§) point to the Decisions Review.*

---

## 1. One-frame orientation

The generator is the detector's primitive pointed outward — same freezer, same drift measure, same reconciliation enum (CORROBORATED / CONTESTED / THIN, opposite polarity) — with **one governing asymmetry**: the detector is *structurally* safe (its vocabulary is GO-LOOK and silence; it has no verb for "acquire"), while the generator's whole job is to **produce new things to want** — which is exactly what the over-trading defense exists to contain. Its safety, in the doc's own words, "cannot be inherited; it must be a held habit" (§1).

Everything below follows from taking that one sentence seriously.

---

## 2. What's strongest — protect it

Two moves are the reason this is more than a web-scanner. Guard them:

- **ETF-as-ontology (§2) is the keystone** — a genuine architectural insight, not a convenience. It hands you nodes / mandate / curator / boundary for free, and it *dissolves* the "new node floods every edge" problem by cleanly separating **new-edge** detection (inside tracked baskets — home turf for a co-occurrence graph) from **new-node** detection (rare, curated ETF launches).
- **Three streams at different consensus positions, with the lag between them as the payload (§3), is the intellectual core.** Velocity-toward-consensus is *early by construction*. Stripped to one sentence, the defensible heart of the whole system is: *measure how fast a linkage travels discourse → rebalancing → launch.*

---

## 3. The one decision worth making now: Fork A (§14A) → deterministic-only v1

§15 is right that the tempting middle — build the expensive discourse stream and then run it reactively — is incoherent (you pay for the hard part and throw away its only advantage, being earliest). But I'd go further than "option (ii) is a respectable v1":

**Deterministic-only is the *disciplined* v1, and the reason is your own build order.** The detector was built deterministic-first, and the generator has the identical layering:

| Detector (built in this order) | Generator equivalent | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Banks A / A′** — FRED regime gauges + look-through, no LLM | **Rebalancing (N-PORT) + launch streams** | deterministic, curated, high-precision, low-fabrication |
| **Bank B** — agentic scouts, cite-or-fault | **Discourse-edge scouts** | noisy, high-recall, expensive, fabrication-prone |
| **Bank C** — reconciliation | **Cross-stream reconciliation** | same enum, opposite polarity |
| **Bank D** — OOD / novelty | **Unrecognized event types** (the fold's Bank D) | novelty in the tool's own categories |

The discourse stream is simultaneously the **noisiest** (§3: "noisy, high-recall, speculative"), the **most expensive** (the only stream needing multi-agent LLM scouts), and the **most fabrication-prone** — so it is the stream that most floods the one resource the design correctly names as the true bottleneck: **the slow human oracle** (§4, the throughput mismatch). For a v1 whose primary failure mode is overwhelming that oracle, leading with the deterministic streams is not a compromise — it is the move that respects the constraint the design itself identifies as the core risk.

**Sequencing:**

- **v1 — deterministic-only.** Rebalancing (N-PORT) + launches. Sit at the rebalancing/launch clock: late, but *"consensus-but-true is fine for a 17-year owner"* is your own line. Build the adjudication muscle on manageable volume. *(Already underway: SPDR XL-series + thematic SPDRs as the universe, etfdb categories as the cheap seed layer — i.e. this is the direction in flight, not a detour.)*
- **v2 — add cadence-capture discourse.** The §15 insight, and it's the right one: freeze raw discourse cheaply on a cadence (single-pass, stored unprocessed), and defer the expensive multi-agent interpretation to triggered moments or backward-thaws. Add it **once the oracle has proven it can handle the firehose** — not before.

Same layering principle you already trusted for the detector: deterministic core first, agentic layers second. The fork isn't cost-vs-interpretability; it's **build order**, and build order says deterministic first.

## 4. The one structural addition: make the pipeline the wall

The detector earned its safety through *structure*; the generator asks you to hold a *habit* where the detector held a *wall*. §12 gestures at the fix (the **detector's break-trigger**, not price, governs what you do with a surfaced lead) but stops short of the structural version:

**A generated lead must enter the *same* candidate pipeline as any other holding, with no shortcut.** It cannot become a position by being interesting — it has to earn a written thesis (Role / Thesis / What it actually is / Checkpoints / Break-it triggers / Sizing note), defined break-triggers, and survive-a-50%-drawdown sizing, exactly like GRID or XBI. **Make the pipeline itself the wall.**

That converts a chunk of the generator's safety from *habitual* back to *structural*: "the generator surfaced something exciting" and "I hold it" are then separated by the identical discipline that governs everything else in the book. Worth writing into §1 as the structural complement to the held habit, and into the decisions ledger.

---

## 5. The permanent limit to keep named

§5's **"reconstructed path, never demonstrated cause"** is the crucial humility, and it's stated correctly: the fossil record is read-only, so you can thaw backward and *observe* precursors but never *prove* a linkage was inevitable — unlike Lenski, who regrows a frozen ancestor and re-runs evolution forward. The append-only schema makes this a *property* rather than a disclaimer you have to remember: you can't edit old verdicts, so you stay honestly scoreable.

But it is a limit to **live with, not solve.** A read-only record is perfectly designed to let hindsight draw an elegant, inevitable line backward from any outcome — and the *interpretation* layer (the fold) is exactly where confirmation bias keeps trying to enter. The defense is the scoring discipline: grading the `no`s that later sprouted `launch`es, on the record. It only works if you actually run it.

---

## 6. Net

Coherent design, one organizing principle — **split cheap-always-on from expensive-triggered, freeze the substrate, defer the interpretation, let the human ratify** — which is the same principle as the detector. One primitive, two faces; that's the point, and the coherence is real.

- **Decision to make now:** Fork A, resolved toward the disciplined **deterministic-only v1** (already in flight with the SPDR series + etfdb), with cadence-capture discourse held for v2.
- **Structural addition to make:** **the same pipeline is the wall** — no shortcut from "surfaced" to "held."
- **Limit to keep named:** reconstructed-path-not-demonstrated-cause, defended by the scoring discipline.
- **Everything else in §14** is parameters, not architecture — set them as you build.
